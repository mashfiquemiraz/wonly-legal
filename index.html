Privacy Policy for Wonly

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Overview

Wonly ("we", "our", or "us") is an independent remittance rate comparison application developed to assist expatriates and migrant workers in South Korea in tracking KRW to BDT exchange rates and fees.

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we do and do not collect, how data is processed, and your rights regarding your data.

2. What Information We DO NOT Collect

Wonly is designed as a privacy-first, informational utility:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide your real name, phone number, or residential address.
  • No Financial or Banking Data: Wonly is not a money transfer operator or financial processor. We never request, process, or store credit card details, bank account numbers, passwords, or transaction records.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not track or request GPS location data.

3. Information We Collect and Why

To provide specific application features, we process minimal technical data:

  • Push Notification Tokens (FCM): If you choose to set a Rate Alert, the app registers a randomized Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token with our secure backend. This token is used exclusively to send automated push alerts when your target exchange rate is reached. You can revoke this permission anytime via your device settings or delete your alerts within the app.
  • App Feedback / Reports: When you submit a problem report via the app, information you voluntarily provide (e.g., your optional contact email and description of the problem) is transmitted directly through your device's native mail client.
  • Anonymous Diagnostics: Standard technical crash analytics (via Google Firebase Crashlytics) may be used to identify software crashes and optimize device compatibility. These diagnostics are completely anonymized and cannot be linked to your personal identity.

4. Third-Party Services

Wonly integrates with trusted third-party services that adhere to rigorous privacy standards:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging & Analytics): Used for delivering rate alert push notifications and monitoring stability. View Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • External Remittance Providers: Wonly provides comparison data for licensed services (E9PAY, GmoneyTrans, CoinShot, Hanpass, SentBe, Cross, GME Remit, Utransfer, JRF Korea). Tapping an external link opens their official website or service in your browser or application. We do not transmit any personal information to these providers.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all API communications between your device and our server.

Alert preferences and associated FCM tokens are retained on the server only for the duration of the alert. Once a one-time alert is triggered and delivered, or when you delete an alert from the app, it is promptly removed.

6. Children's Privacy

Wonly is not directed toward individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
